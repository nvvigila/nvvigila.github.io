About Me

Computational chemist and soon-to-be PhD graduate specializing in electronic-structure theory, density functional theory (DFT), molecular dynamics (MD), and multiscale atomistic modelling of functional materials. My research combines quantum-mechanical calculations with large-scale simulations to develop predictive models that connect atomic-scale interactions with macroscopic material properties.

I have extensive experience in force-field development, interface modelling, high-performance computing, and scientific programming using Python. An 18-month research appointment at the California Institute of Technology strengthened my expertise in advanced computational methodologies and collaborative interdisciplinary research.

I am motivated to develop next-generation computational approaches that integrate physics-based simulations, artificial intelligence, and data-driven modelling to accelerate the discovery and design of advanced materials.

Projects

Quantum-Mechanics-Derived Force Field Development for Poly(vinylidene fluoride) (PVDF)

Developed an all-atom force field directly from density functional theory calculations to accurately reproduce the structural, vibrational, mechanical, and dielectric properties of PVDF for large-scale molecular dynamics simulations.

First-Principles Force Field Development for Iron Oxides (Hematite, Magnetite, Maghemite, and Wüstite)

Parameterized transferable atomistic force fields for multiple iron oxide phases using DFT calculations to enable predictive simulations of structural stability, surface chemistry, and interfacial interactions.

Multiscale Atomistic Modelling of PVDF–Iron Oxide Nanocomposites

Investigated the influence of nanoparticle surface chemistry and organic ligands on polymer–nanoparticle interfaces, mechanical reinforcement, and dielectric behaviour through large-scale molecular dynamics simulations.

Surface Functionalization of γ-Fe₂O₃ Nanoparticles with Carboxylate Ligands

Combined density functional theory and molecular dynamics to study ligand adsorption, surface energetics, crystal morphology, and polymer adhesion on different maghemite facets.

Interfacial Adhesion and Surface Energy of Polymer–Nanoparticle Systems

Calculated surface energies, adsorption energies, work of adhesion, and interfacial energies to understand how molecular interactions govern composite performance.

Atomistic Force Fields for Functional Materials

Developed transferable quantum-mechanics-based force fields suitable for large-scale simulations and future integration with machine learning approaches for accelerated materials discovery.
